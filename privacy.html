HMB NEXUS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information HMB NEXUS may process when you use the Discord application/bot and how that information is used, stored, and deleted.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the commands and features you use, HMB NEXUS may receive basic Discord information made available through Discord, such as your Discord user ID, username/display name, avatar information, server ID, channel ID, role/permission information, command interactions, and information needed to execute a requested command.

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

HMB NEXUS does not intentionally collect Discord passwords, payment card numbers, or authentication tokens from users. Never send your Discord token, password, or other secret credentials to the bot or its operators.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to provide requested commands, respond to interactions, perform moderation functions, maintain bot security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

4. Stored Data

Some commands may require limited persistent data, such as moderation records, warnings, economy/game progress, configuration settings, or other command-related state. Only data reasonably needed for the relevant feature should be stored.

5. Retention and Deletion

We aim to retain stored information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the feature, security, moderation, or operational purpose for which it was collected. Where technically available, users or server administrators may request deletion of data associated with HMB NEXUS.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or third-party services used to operate the bot, such as hosting providers or APIs, only as reasonably necessary to provide the service or maintain security.

7. Discord and Third-Party Services

HMB NEXUS operates on Discord and is subject to Discord’s platform rules and privacy practices. Discord separately processes information under its own policies. Third-party services used by individual commands may have their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information and access credentials. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

HMB NEXUS does not knowingly request or collect information beyond what is needed to operate its Discord features. Users must comply with Discord’s age requirements and applicable laws.

10. Your Requests

You may contact the HMB NEXUS team to ask what information associated with HMB NEXUS may be stored, or to request deletion where applicable.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the bot, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will show a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the HMB NEXUS team through the official HMB NEXUS Discord support/community channel or the account that operates the application.

HMB NEXUS is an independent third-party Discord application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc.
